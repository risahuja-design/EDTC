About EDTC

Who We Are and What We Stand For

The Electronics Design and Testing Centre (EDTC) is a premier organization dedicated to advancing the field of electronics through innovative design solutions, comprehensive testing services, and cutting-edge research. With a commitment to excellence and precision, we serve as a trusted partner for businesses and institutions seeking to push the boundaries of electronic technology.

Our state-of-the-art facilities house advanced equipment and testing laboratories, enabling us to deliver world-class services across the entire electronics development lifecycle. From concept to production, we provide the expertise and resources needed to transform ideas into reality.

Products

Innovative Solutions for Modern Electronics

EDTC develops and delivers specialized products designed to address the complex challenges of modern electronics design and testing. Our product portfolio combines advanced technology with practical functionality to provide effective solutions for diverse applications.

PCB

Custom PCB Design Solutions

Comprehensive PCB design services from concept to production, including multi-layer boards, high-frequency designs, and specialized applications. Our solutions incorporate signal integrity analysis, thermal management, and EMC compliance.

  • High-speed digital design
  • RF and microwave circuits
  • Power electronics layouts
  • Flex and rigid-flex designs
TEST

Automated Testing Systems

State-of-the-art automated test equipment (ATE) and custom test fixtures designed to streamline your quality assurance processes. Our systems provide comprehensive coverage with reduced testing time.

  • Functional testing platforms
  • Boundary scan solutions
  • In-circuit testing systems
  • Custom test fixtures

Specialized Testing Services

  • Environmental Testing: Temperature, humidity, vibration, and shock testing
  • EMC/EMI Testing: Conducted and radiated emissions and immunity testing
  • Safety Testing: Electrical safety and compliance certification support
  • Reliability Testing: Accelerated life testing and failure analysis
  • Signal Integrity: High-speed digital signal analysis and characterization
  • Power Testing: Efficiency, transient response, and load regulation testing

Consulting Services

Our experienced engineers provide expert consulting on design optimization, technology selection, compliance requirements, and best practices. We help clients navigate complex technical challenges and make informed decisions.
